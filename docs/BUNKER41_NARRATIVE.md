# The Black Signal of Bunker 41

This optional, deterministic narrative arc is loaded from `data/bunker41_events.json`. It begins as a Salt Flats omen, becomes a Glass Wastes bunker choice, branches through the Underrail, and replaces the final Warden-to-gate sequence with Bunker Forty-One-aware scenes.

## Chain map

1. **Static Beneath the Salt** establishes a recording, answered signal, or deliberate silence.
2. **The Rusted Hatch** branches into Mara Venn’s maintenance route, the Cinder Wives’ forced-entry route, or an Ash Choir trail left after refusal.
3. **The Cartographer Under Glass** and **The Last Evacuation** establish the human cost and the Mercy Key; **The Door That Breathes** contains the Hush Sovereign’s explicitly telegraphed lethal route.
4. **The Fragment That Calls**, **The Ash Choir’s Procession**, and **The Green Platform** carry surviving choices into the Underrail.
5. **The Chapel Car**, **The False Welcome**, and **The Ledger of the Living** determine whether the survivor reaches the Citadel with a key, testimony, rescued people, Choir records, or chosen silence.
6. **The Warden Remembers** and **The Door That Closes** turn that history into four final approaches: Silence, Mercy, Witness, or Ash.

## Narrative rules

- The Hush Sovereign’s lethal option names the bent pressure door, crushed steel, furnace breath, broad handprints, and the creature’s rhythm before the player can enter it. It is never an unexplained instant death.
- Bunker scenes use direct narrative choices rather than new stats, probability rules, equipment, or monetization. Existing game systems remain unchanged.
- Delayed chapters use persistent narrative flags plus region timing. An earned Bunker continuation has priority over ordinary Underrail filler, except when the region’s guaranteed supply event must be selected.
- The chain remains optional. A run that misses its omen or refuses the bunker can still complete the normal journey.
