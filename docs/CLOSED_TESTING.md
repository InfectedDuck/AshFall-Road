# Closed-test guide and feedback form

## Tester instructions

Thank you for testing Ashfall Road. This is a strict-permadeath, offline text RPG. Please remain opted into the Google Play closed test until the developer confirms the test is complete.

1. Install only through the supplied Google Play opt-in link.
2. Play at least two sessions on different days.
3. In one session, turn on airplane mode before launching and confirm the run remains playable.
4. Do not avoid failure. If the survivor dies, start a second run and see whether the cause taught you something useful.
5. Try Inventory, an equipment change, a consumable, a checked choice, combat, Settings, and Android Back.
6. Report crashes, frozen controls, lost progress, resurrected survivors, duplicated rewards, unreadable text, or clipped controls immediately.
7. Feedback and reviews must be honest. No rating or positive review is required.

Do not share screenshots containing personal notifications, email addresses, or other private device information.

## Producer tracking row

Record one row per session in a private sheet:

`tester | build | device | minutes | runs | first-death-minute | furthest-region | replayed | P0/P1 issue | understood-death | notes`

Minimum evidence before applying for production access:

- 15-20 recruited testers, with at least 12 continuously opted in for 14 complete days.
- At least eight useful completed feedback forms.
- At least two sessions requested from each active tester.
- Multiple Android versions and screen sizes represented.
- A dated build/change log showing what feedback produced which fixes.
