# Ashfall Road — first itch.io playtest

Prepared 15 September 2026. This is a proposed launch approach, not evidence of player enjoyment or release certification.

## Positioning and page copy

**Short description:** A survival text RPG about dangerous choices, scarce supplies, and the people you meet on the road to safety.

**Opening copy:**

The Citadel promises clean air and shelter. Reaching it means crossing a broken world—and deciding what you can afford to carry.

Read the situation, choose your risks, manage your supplies, and face the consequences. Who will you help along the way?

**Release label:** Free public playtest / in development. Specify the actual supported platforms, controls, and known issues of the uploaded build. Add a measured playtime after observing complete runs.

Show three real screenshots: a story choice with its costs; inventory/equipment; and a consequential result or combat scene. Label promotional art as cover art. Keep the text RPG format clear.

## First audience

Aim first for 10 people who play the opening and provide specific feedback. This is a working target, not a popularity forecast.

1. Verify the export launches on a clean machine and test save/resume and a complete journey. Previous automated checks reported save/recovery failures; determine which are environment restrictions and which are product defects before advertising release stability.
2. Publish an accessible free playtest. A browser demo is worth considering if the Godot web export works reliably; otherwise begin with the working downloadable build.
3. Share one actual dilemma or a brief gameplay clip in relevant interactive-fiction and indie-game communities that allow promotion. Invite people to try the first 10 minutes. Offer to play another developer's game and give thoughtful feedback.
4. Ask three questions: Where did you become confused? Which person or choice stayed with you? Where did you stop, and why?
5. Fix the most repeated obstacle. Post a short devlog showing the concrete improvement, and invite the testers back.

**Invitation to copy:**

I'm making Ashfall Road, a post-apocalyptic survival text RPG. I'm looking for a few people to try the first 10 minutes of the free playtest and tell me where they got confused, which choice they cared about, and where they stopped. [Add itch.io link]

Use accurate tags such as text-based, interactive fiction, survival, post-apocalyptic, and choices matter when available and supported by the actual build. Do not market romance until it is implemented and tested.

## What to improve before adding lots of content

- Opening: establish a person with an immediate need, offer a meaningful decision early, and show its effect soon enough for the player to notice.
- Relationships: use distinct goals, disagreement, humor, useful cooperation, and quiet companionship. Let kindness sometimes work.
- Payoffs: finish a local promise. Show what changed for someone because of the player's decision.
- Presentation: make the text readable and costs understandable, and allow fast reveal. Use screenshots to demonstrate this.
- Measure manually first: visitors/downloads give a partial picture; tester reports tell you whether people understood or enjoyed the experience. Do not assume a download equals a completed session.

## Optional romance experiment

A compelling, attractive character can fit the game. Romance should earn its screen time through writing.

Try one optional adult relationship with three beats: practical cooperation, an honest disagreement or vulnerable moment, then a mutual choice about the future. Include friendship and refusal routes, and a satisfying non-romantic outcome. Affection should not be purchased simply by donating items. These are proposed additions, not current game features.

Use a small playtest to see whether readers want more before building multiple romance routes. The cover must reflect the game's real experience.
