# Asset provenance

## Generated environment art

The seven PNG backgrounds under `assets/backgrounds/` were generated for this project with OpenAI's image-generation tool on 30 August 2026. They are original project assets; no external reference images were supplied. All prompts requested portrait mobile-game environment art, quiet dark areas for overlaid UI, teen-rated non-graphic content, and no text, logos, or watermark.

- `region_1_outskirts.png`: cracked highway, ruined suburbs, abandoned vehicles, storm haze; muted ochre and slate.
- `region_2_salt_flats.png`: bleached salt basin, broken elevated road, utility towers, heat haze; ivory, rust, and pale cyan.
- `region_3_marshes.png`: flooded rural road, reeds, drowned structures, contaminated green light; teal, moss, and charcoal.
- `region_4_industrial.png`: abandoned factories, pipe bridges, smokestacks, freight tracks; charcoal and rust orange.
- `region_5_glass_wastes.png`: fused glass desert, damaged solar arrays, distant observatory, violet storm.
- `region_6_underrail.png`: flooded underground rail interchange, cables, tunnel lights; steel blue and restrained red.
- `region_7_citadel.png`: fortified research tower, ruined crater city, broken causeway, warm beacon.

## Code-native art and audio

- `assets/ui/icon.svg` is a project-created vector icon.
- UI tones are synthesized at runtime by `scripts/services/sound_service.gd`; no third-party audio sample is bundled.
- Equipment and inventory category silhouettes are drawn at runtime by `scripts/ui/item_icon.gd`; no third-party icon artwork is bundled.
- `assets/items/inventory_weapons_v1.png`, `inventory_gear_v1.png`, `inventory_supplies_v1.png`, and `inventory_utilities_v1.png` were generated for this project with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool on 14 September 2026, without external references. Together they are four transparent pixel-art atlases covering all 50 current item IDs: weapons; worn gear and accessories; packs and consumables; then medicines, ammunition, scrap, and rope. `item_icon.gd` uses nearest-neighbor sampling and maps each stable item ID to its own cell. Dedicated future per-item PNGs retain priority.
- `assets/ui/ui_symbols_v1.png` was generated for this project with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool on 14 September 2026, without external references. It is a transparent 4 by 4 pixel-art atlas for health, food, pressures, five stats, navigation, Chronicle, settings, inventory, status, rest, and Press On. `ui_icon.gd` renders it with nearest-neighbor sampling while buttons retain text and accessibility names.

## Archived Blender enemy portrait experiment — 8 September 2026

Thirteen experimental enemy PNGs were pre-rendered from original procedural mesh scenes authored by Codex in `tools/blender/build_enemy_portraits.py` and rendered locally with Blender 5.2.1 LTS / Cycles. The user rejected their appearance and chose image-generated portraits. The runtime installation was rolled back: the three original enemy PNGs were restored byte-for-byte and the ten newly filled slots returned to their prior state, with all rollback checks passing. No third-party mesh, texture, add-on, or external image reference was imported. Editable scenes and experimental renders remain archived in the Godot-ignored `art_sources/blender/` directory and are not selected game assets.

The design source was `docs/ENEMY_PORTRAIT_PROMPTS_V4.md` (`enemy-portrait-v4.2`) and `docs/ENEMY_DESIGN_BIBLE.md`, with selected variants from the Bog Raider and Crow generation records. The output was a first pass of faceted portrait sculptures, without animation rigs. Final visual review: rejected; image-generated portrait production remains the selected direction.

Godot's project-authored `tools/blender/normalize_portrait.gd` converts renders to opaque 64×64 PNGs using nearest-neighbor sampling, one reserved twenty-color palette, and flat `#20272B`. The initial per-image median-cut pass was replaced after small accent colors were lost in thumbnail review. The previous runtime `enemy_stalker.png`, `enemy_dogs.png`, and `enemy_toll.png` are preserved byte-for-byte in `art_sources/blender/previous_runtime/`, with hashes in `installation.json`; their earlier provenance above still applies to those originals. All older high-resolution portrait sources remain preserved. See `docs/BLENDER_ENEMY_ASSETS.md` for inventory, rebuild instructions, verification, and limitations.

## Bundled fonts

- `assets/fonts/Literata.ttf` is Literata, downloaded from the official Google Fonts repository and used for narrative prose. It is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the bundled licence is `assets/fonts/OFL-Literata.txt`.
- `assets/fonts/Inter.ttf` is Inter, downloaded from the official Google Fonts repository and used for interface controls, HUD labels, numbers, and combat logs. It is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the bundled licence is `assets/fonts/OFL-Inter.txt`.
- The font binaries and licence files are packaged locally; neither font requires a runtime network request.
