# Mega-Chain Expansion

The expansion is being added in independently playable, validated waves. Each wave is a supplemental JSON pack merged by `ContentRepository`, so it preserves base-event IDs, save compatibility, offline play, and strict permadeath.

## Wave A — Mara Venn and the Rust-Sea

`data/rustsea_events.json` contains the first eight playable nodes of the expansion:

1. A Voice on the Green Line
2. The Quay of Names
3. The False Coordinates
4. The Station Without Arrivals
5. The Salt Crown's Wake
6. The Cartographer's Debt
7. The Map Made of Voices
8. The Last Coordinate

It is available only in the Underrail after a relevant Bunker Forty-One choice. The arc reads the existing Bunker flags for Mara, the preserved signal, and the Mercy Key. It writes durable Rust-Sea flags for later waves, including saved or betrayed Mara, copied names, Ash Choir friction, hidden survivors, the Sunken Spire route, and named witnesses.

Two choices deliberately lead to strict permadeath: entering the warm hollow beneath the Salt Crown. Both scenes describe the creature's vibration, bent metal, warm breath, and warning messages before the player commits.

## Future waves

- **Wave B:** The Mercy Key, Cinder Wives, the hidden refuge, and the Lantern Jurisdiction.
- **Wave C:** Ash Choir defectors, the masked child, the Drowned Surveyor, and the Rust-Sea tribunal.
- **Convergence:** The Sunken Spire, the Bellmother, the continental alarm, and new evidence-driven meanings for the Citadel finale.

Expansion routes may make more than three direct narrative transitions when they are earned by a prior unique choice. Each branch must still have an intentional exit to the ordinary journey, persistent flags for future consequences, clear warnings before lethal paths, and automated coverage of every handoff.
